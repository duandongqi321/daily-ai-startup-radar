# Scoring Rubric

Score each candidate out of 100. Use the score to support selection, not to pretend precision.

## Dimensions

| Dimension | Points | What Good Looks Like |
|---|---:|---|
| Signal freshness and credibility | 15 | Recent, sourceable, material signal from credible sources |
| AI centrality | 15 | AI is core to the product, workflow, infrastructure, or data advantage |
| Customer pain and use case clarity | 15 | Clear buyer/user, clear job to be done, non-trivial pain |
| Business model and monetization | 10 | Plausible path to revenue or strategic value |
| Founder/team fit | 10 | Public evidence of relevant domain, technical, product, or distribution strength |
| Traction or distribution signal | 10 | Customers, usage, pilots, developer adoption, partnerships, or strong launch momentum |
| Defensibility and differentiation | 10 | Data, workflow lock-in, technical edge, regulatory/domain know-how, distribution, brand, or ecosystem position |
| Market timing and potential | 10 | Growing budget, behavior shift, regulation, platform shift, or urgent market need |
| Personal Lens fit | 5 | Directly useful to the user's goals, markets, sectors, or opportunity style |

## Rating Bands

- 85-100: Radar Pick. Strong signal and high learning value.
- 70-84: Strong Watch. Worth tracking closely.
- 55-69: Useful Mention. Interesting but incomplete or less personally relevant.
- 40-54: Low-Confidence Watch. Needs more evidence.
- Below 40: Usually skip unless the user specifically asked about it.

## Confidence Grade

Assign a separate confidence grade:

- High: multiple credible sources, clear recent signal, low ambiguity.
- Medium: at least one credible source but some missing context.
- Low: early, thin, social-only, stealthy, or geographically ambiguous.

## Risk Tags

Use risk tags to keep analysis honest:

- "unclear buyer"
- "crowded category"
- "weak differentiation"
- "regulatory risk"
- "platform dependency"
- "capital intensive"
- "data access risk"
- "long enterprise sales cycle"
- "unclear AI depth"
- "source confidence low"

## Scoring Notes

- Do not let funding amount dominate the score.
- A small company with a sharp workflow and strong Personal Lens can outrank a heavily funded but generic company.
- If the company is exciting but evidence is thin, give a promising score with lower confidence rather than inflating certainty.
- If the company is not clearly a startup, reduce score unless the signal directly affects startup opportunity discovery.
